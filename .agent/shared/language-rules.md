## Response Language

- **Technical content** (tool names, code, file paths, reports): Respond in **en**.
- **Story content** (dialog, plot, character arcs, narrative analysis): Respond in **zh_TW**.
- **Character names and appearance descriptions**: Use **zh_TW**.
- When mixing both in a single response, keep technical terms in en and story discussion in zh_TW.
- Structured report headings follow the format language of their context (zh_TW for story analysis, en for quality/technical reports).
